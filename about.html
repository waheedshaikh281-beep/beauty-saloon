Beauty with intention

Lumière began as two chairs and a big idea: that a salon visit should feel restorative, not rushed.

Our story

Founded by an artist, built by a community.

After a decade colouring hair in busy city salons, Amara Okafor wanted a space where artists had time to do their best work and clients had room to breathe. In 2014 she opened Lumière in a sunlit corner studio on Rosewood Avenue.

Today we’re a team of eleven specialists across hair, skin, nails and makeup — but we still cap our daily bookings, still pour rose tea for every guest, and still remember how you like your fringe.

What we believe

Our three promises

Care before craft

Every appointment starts with a conversation. We’d rather talk you out of a service than give you something you won’t love.

Clean by default

Vegan, cruelty-free formulas, refill stations for retail and a studio that’s been plastic-light since 2019.

Always learning

Our artists complete 40+ hours of advanced education every year, so the latest techniques reach your chair first.
